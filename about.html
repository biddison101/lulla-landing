What Lulla is

An anxiety app that listens
to your body first.

Most calming apps hand you a library of techniques and leave you to figure it out. That's the last thing you can do when you're in the middle of it — heart pounding, thoughts spiraling, or feeling completely numb.

Lulla works differently. It starts with one question: what is your body doing right now? Based on your answer, it routes you to the one technique that's actually matched to that physical state — not a generic suggestion, but a clinically grounded response to what's happening in your nervous system.

It takes under 60 seconds. No account. Nothing leaves your phone.

The science

Every technique has
a real reason.

These aren't wellness platitudes. They're the interventions that have clinical evidence behind them — and Lulla matches each one to the specific physical state it's designed for.

Heart racing

4-8 Breathing — vagus nerve activation

An exhale twice as long as the inhale directly stimulates the vagus nerve, triggering the parasympathetic response that slows heart rate. Used in clinical settings for acute cardiac anxiety.

Can't breathe

Box Breathing — CO₂ rebalancing

Equal counts in, hold, out, hold interrupts the hyperventilation cycle and restores oxygen-to-CO₂ balance. Adopted by the US Navy SEALs for use in high-stress combat situations.

Mind won't stop

5-4-3-2-1 Grounding — sensory interruption

Directing attention to immediate sensory input activates the prefrontal cortex and interrupts the rumination loop. Standard protocol in trauma-informed cognitive behavioral therapy.

Numb or floating

Somatic Grounding — gentle re-embodiment

Dissociative states require stillness, not stimulation. Slow, deliberate pressure anchors — feet on the floor, hands on lap — re-establish body awareness without triggering further dissociation.

Shaky or restless

Somatic Release — adrenaline discharge

Restlessness is unspent adrenaline. Short, intentional movements allow the stress hormone cycle to complete, preventing the re-activation loop that keeps anxiety elevated.

Overwhelmed

Co-Regulation — zero-interaction calming

When emotionally flooded, executive function is offline — choosing is too much. This path auto-advances with no required input, providing a regulated rhythm to synchronise with.

Privacy

No catch.

Mental health data is the most sensitive data there is. We designed Lulla from the ground up to never need a server — because the safest place for your data is your own device.
