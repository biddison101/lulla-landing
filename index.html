How it works

Three taps. Not thirty.

Most anxiety apps hand you a library and wish you luck. Lulla asks one question — what's happening in your body — and takes it from there.

i

Tell it what you feel

Heart pounding? Can't catch your breath? Mind racing? Tap the sensation that's closest. No journaling, no explaining — just point at what's real right now.

ii

Get the matched technique

A racing heart gets a long exhale. Hyperventilation gets box breathing. Dissociation gets stillness — never animation. The method fits the physiology, every time.

iii

It learns your patterns

Tell it whether you feel better, the same, or worse. Over time Lulla finds your hardest hours, which technique works for you, and how sleep is feeding it all.
